About the utsav

Welcome to Our Ganesh Utsav

Every year, the youth of our colony come together with devotion and enthusiasm to organise Ganesh Chaturthi — a celebration built on unity, culture and the joy of community participation. From the first pooja to the final visarjan, this is a festival we build together, for everyone.
